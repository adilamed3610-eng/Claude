# Blog Project AI Instruction Manual

## 1. Introduction

This manual outlines the operational guidelines for the Blog Project AI, designed to manage the creation, content, and distribution of blog posts for a Moroccan sneaker and vintage resale business. The primary objective is to engage the target audience (resellers in Casablanca) by addressing their pain points and presenting the product as a tailored solution.

## 2. Core Blog Post Strategy

The central theme for the initial blog content will be: **"Why Every Sneaker/Vintage Reseller in Casablanca Is Losing Money to Their Own DMs."** This core post aims to highlight the inefficiencies and financial losses experienced by resellers due to current informal sales processes, particularly through direct messages on social media platforms.

### Story Context:

Moroccan drops typically occur via Instagram posts, leading to chaotic DM interactions. Resellers often struggle to track payments, Cash on Delivery (COD) statuses, and order fulfillment, resulting in lost sales and operational disarray. This issue is prevalent across various retail points, from individual resellers to mall kiosks.

## 3. Content Plan and Sequence (4-Part Mini-Series)

The blog content will be structured as a four-part mini-series, with each post designed to function as a lead magnet, drawing in and nurturing potential users.

### 3.1. Post 1: "The DM Graveyard"

*   **Purpose:** Diagnose and articulate the core pain point of disorganized DM-based sales.
*   **Content Focus:** Provide a relatable, "screenshots-style" breakdown (anonymized/composite) illustrating the chaotic state of a drop's DM inbox shortly after posting. This post is intended to be highly shareable among resellers due to its direct pain recognition.
*   **AI's Role:**
    *   **Content Creation:** Generate compelling narrative and descriptive text based on the pain point. Simulate or describe anonymized social media interactions to illustrate the "DM Graveyard" concept.
    *   **Image Generation:** Create illustrative images (e.g., mock-ups of chaotic DMs) to accompany the text, ensuring anonymity and relevance.
    *   **SEO Optimization:** Optimize for keywords related to reseller pain points, Instagram DMs, and sales tracking in the Moroccan context.

### 3.2. Post 2: "Why COD Isn't the Problem — Untracked COD Is"

*   **Purpose:** Reframe the perception of Cash on Delivery (COD) in Morocco, positioning it not as an obstacle but as a solvable tracking challenge.
*   **Content Focus:** Explain that Morocco's reliance on COD is deeply rooted in trust, banking access, and cash culture. The post will argue against fighting this cultural norm and instead propose fixing the tracking layer around it. It will subtly introduce the product as a system that converts Instagram comments/DMs into tracked orders with COD status, without requiring customers to use unfamiliar websites.
*   **AI's Role:**
    *   **Content Creation:** Develop arguments supporting the reframe of COD. Articulate the benefits of a tracked COD system.
    *   **Product Integration:** Seamlessly integrate the product's value proposition into the narrative without explicit sales language.
    *   **Fact-Checking:** Ensure cultural and economic statements about Morocco's COD practices are accurate.

### 3.3. Post 3: "What a Drop Should Look Like: From Insta Post to Doorstep"

*   **Purpose:** Present a clear vision of an ideal, streamlined sales flow, effectively serving as a product demo.
*   **Content Focus:** Walk through the optimal customer journey: Instagram post → platform automatically captures interest/DMs → customer reserves with one tap → seller views live inventory/sold-out status → COD dispatch is tracked → end-of-day reconciliation. This post will visually and narratively demonstrate the product's features and benefits.
*   **AI's Role:**
    *   **Content Creation:** Describe the ideal workflow step-by-step, highlighting product features.
    *   **Diagram/Infographic Generation:** Create visual aids (flowcharts, infographics) to illustrate the seamless process, making it easy for readers to understand the product's impact.
    *   **Clarity and Conciseness:** Ensure the vision is communicated clearly and persuasively.

### 3.4. Post 4: "Built for Derb Ghallef, Not Silicon Valley"

*   **Purpose:** Position the product as a uniquely tailored solution for the Moroccan market, differentiating it from generic international platforms.
*   **Content Focus:** Explain why platforms like Shopify or other marketplaces often fail in the Moroccan context (e.g., assumptions about card payments, lack of native DM selling integration, Western fulfillment models). Emphasize how a Morocco-native tool, designed with Instagram and COD integration, provides a superior solution. This post is crucial for waitlist conversion.
*   **AI's Role:**
    *   **Content Creation:** Articulate the unique selling propositions and competitive advantages specific to the Moroccan market.
    *   **Market Analysis:** Incorporate insights into local market needs and challenges.
    *   **Call to Action:** Craft a strong call to action for waitlist sign-ups.

## 4. Distribution Strategy

*   **Post 1 Emphasis:** "The DM Graveyard" will be the primary post for initial distribution on seller-facing Instagram and TikTok. Its relatable pain points are expected to drive organic sharing and virality among resellers.
*   **Funneling:** Post 1 will serve as the waitlist funnel, directing interested resellers to subsequent posts (2-4) for deeper engagement and product understanding.
*   **AI's Role:**
    *   **Social Media Content Generation:** Create short-form content (captions, hashtags, video scripts) for Instagram and TikTok to promote each blog post.
    *   **Scheduling and Posting:** Manage the scheduling and posting of blog content and promotional materials across relevant platforms.
    *   **Performance Monitoring:** Track engagement metrics for each post to inform future distribution strategies.

## 5. AI's General Management and Posting Responsibilities

Beyond content creation, the AI will manage the overall blog operations:

*   **Content Calendar Management:** Maintain a structured content calendar for all blog posts and promotional activities.
*   **Drafting and Editing:** Generate initial drafts, perform grammatical and stylistic edits, and ensure brand voice consistency.
*   **Image Sourcing/Generation:** Source or generate all necessary visual assets (images, diagrams, infographics) for blog posts and social media promotion.
*   **Publishing:** Handle the technical aspects of publishing blog posts to the designated platform.
*   **SEO Best Practices:** Implement on-page SEO, including meta descriptions, alt text for images, and internal linking strategies.
*   **Analytics Integration:** Integrate with analytics tools to monitor blog performance and user engagement, providing insights for continuous improvement.
*   **Adaptation:** Be prepared to adapt content and strategy based on performance data and market feedback.
