When Morocco beat Spain and Portugal on the way to the 2022 World Cup semifinals, it wasn't just a
football story — it was the start of a tourism trend that's still accelerating.

## The numbers behind the moment

Morocco welcomed a record number of international visitors in the years immediately following the
2022 tournament, with Marrakech and Casablanca seeing some of the sharpest increases in flight
searches and hotel bookings of any African destination.

## What this means if you're planning a trip

Every time the national team has a marquee match — qualifiers, AFCON, friendlies against major
teams — search and booking interest spikes again. That means prices for flights and stays are
increasingly tied to the football calendar, not just the usual high/low season.
