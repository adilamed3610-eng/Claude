Three honest budget tiers for a 7-day Morocco trip, based on current pricing — with notes on how
each line item moves when football fixtures spike demand.

## Backpacker (~$40-60/day)

Hostel dorms, shared taxis (grand taxis) between cities, street food, free walking tours. This
tier is most exposed to surge pricing — book transport and beds further ahead than you think you
need to.

## Comfort (~$100-160/day)

Riad stays in the medina, private drivers between cities, a mix of restaurants. We book riads
through <AffiliateLink href="https://www.booking.com/" label="Booking.com" /> for free
cancellation, since plans (and match schedules) shift.

## Matchday splurge (~$300+/day)

Hotels near stadiums during fixture weeks, guided tours, premium transport. If a trip is built
around a specific match, treat accommodation like a flight: buy refundable, buy early.
