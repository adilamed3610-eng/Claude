Morocco, Spain, and Portugal are co-hosting the 2030 FIFA World Cup — and Morocco's matches will be
the centerpiece of one of the biggest tourism moments the country has ever had. If you're even
thinking about going, the planning window starts now, not the month before kickoff.

## Why Morocco, why now

Morocco's bid built on the momentum of the Atlas Lions' run to the 2022 semifinals — the first
African and Arab nation to reach that stage. That single tournament reshaped global perception of
Moroccan football and tourism overnight, and flight searches to Casablanca and Marrakech spiked
every time the team won a knockout match.

## What to plan for first

- **Visas**: rules vary a lot by nationality. Start checking your country's requirements early —
  this is the single biggest blocker for last-minute World Cup trips.
- **Accommodation**: host-city hotel prices during major tournaments typically rise 3-5x. Booking
  12+ months out is normal for World Cup travel.
- **Flights**: prices into Casablanca Mohammed V (CMN) move with team performance news, not just
  the calendar — set fare alerts now.
